What Runix is

The entity you would contract with, and what it actually operates today.

Runix AI Inc is a company incorporated in Wyoming, United States. It builds and operates infrastructure for teams putting AI into production, and sells it directly — there is no reseller between you and the people who run the service. The longer version of why it exists is in the announcement post.

Four products, at deliberately different stages. The statuses below are literal, and they are the same statuses used everywhere else on this site; none of them is aspirational.

  • Runix Router — early access, running traffic today. One OpenAI-compatible endpoint across many providers, with central key custody, per-key quotas, request-level audit and failover between providers. What Router does.
  • Runix Pipeline — in development with design partners. Managed data preparation: ingestion, deduplication, schema-validated extraction, PII masking that fails closed. The six Pipeline stages.
  • Runix Code — in development, waitlist open. An AI coding agent for teams. Not generally available. Code and its waitlist.
  • Runix Comic — in development, waitlist open. An end-to-end studio for comic dramas. Not generally available. Comic and its waitlist.

How we work

What we believe

The hard part of AI is not the demo — it is everything after: keeping services up when providers fail, keeping costs visible as usage grows, and turning messy data into something a model can actually use.

That is the work we love. Runix focuses on a small set of infrastructure problems and solves them properly, so the teams we serve can focus entirely on their product.

  • Reliability is a feature. We design for failure first and publish no availability number we have not yet earned.
  • Transparency builds trust. Clear pricing, itemized usage, honest communication.
  • Long-term over short-term. We would rather keep a customer for years than win a quarter.
  • Written down, not remembered. Decisions live in documents rather than in someone's head — which is also what working here is like.
