How usage stays predictable

Every seat runs against three rolling limits at once. It is the difference between "we lost a month's budget to one bad agent loop" and a plan that lasts exactly as long as it should.

Rolling 5 hours

Burst control

Caps a short spike — an agent stuck in a loop hits the 5-hour ceiling and pauses, instead of draining the pool in one sitting. Resets continuously.

Per week

Pacing

Keeps a single heavy week from consuming the whole month, so usage spreads evenly and every seat gets a fair share of the team's credits.

Per month

The allowance

The seat's total for the billing period. Reaching it pauses that seat until renewal; top up the team pool any time if you need more headroom.

Common questions

What is a credit?

A fixed unit of usage that covers any model or tool at one published rate. You never reason about tokens or per-model dollar prices — frontier models, lighter models, and search / fetch / data tools all meter in the same credit.

What happens at the limit?

Usage pauses until the window resets. Each seat runs against a rolling 5-hour cap, a weekly cap and a monthly allowance — so no runaway agent, no surprise bill. Need more? Top up the pool.

Invoices & bank transfer?

Yes. Card and online payment are instant; for teams we issue invoices with your company name and tax ID, support net terms, and can credit your pool against a redemption code after an offline transfer.

How many seats?

From a handful up to 30–50 and beyond. Credits pool across the team; an admin sets per-seat caps, restricts models, and sees per-seat usage. Tell us your team size.
