02 · Capabilities

Assistance, agency, and adult supervision

AI coding fails teams in two opposite ways: suggestions too generic to trust, or agents too autonomous to audit. Runix Code is designed against both.

Grounded in your repository

Designed so suggestions follow your conventions, not a tutorial's.

  • Codebase awareness — completion and chat that reference your real modules and idioms
  • Editor & terminal — the same assistant in the IDE and on the command line
  • Dependency literacy — answers that respect the versions you actually run

Tasks, not just tab-completes

Agentic where it saves hours; reviewable at every step.

  • Task execution — implement, refactor and test multi-file changes from one instruction
  • Review gates — every change lands as a reviewable diff, never a silent write
  • Test-first habits — changes validated against your test suite before they reach you

Rolled out, not smuggled in

The administrative layer most coding tools leave to the honor system.

  • Team management — seats, per-key limits and usage attribution by developer
  • Model routing — your policies decide which models serve which repositories
  • Data terms in writing — code serves the request; it is not training data
Runix Code is in development. This page describes what is being built, not a shipped product. Waitlist cohorts get access as capabilities land, and their feedback sets the order.

03 · Fit

Who it is for

Built team-first: useful solo, designed for organisations.

Platform & DevEx

07 · Questions

Common questions

What is Runix Code?

An AI coding agent being built for teams: codebase-aware assistance, agentic task execution under review gates, and the administrative layer (seats, attribution, data terms) that makes org-wide rollout sane.

How is it different from a generic assistant?

Context and governance. Suggestions grounded in your repository rather than generic patterns; usage attributed per seat, models routed under your policies, prompts kept inside the boundary you configure.

When can I use it?

It is in development and not generally available. We onboard from the waitlist in small cohorts: tell us your stack and team size and we reply within one business day.

Is my code used for training?

No. Code processed by the product serves your request, is not used to train models and is not sold: the same posture as every Runix product. See Privacy.

Get your team in early

Tell us your stack, your team size and what you want an agent to take off your plate: we reply within one business day with where the product is and when you would get access.
