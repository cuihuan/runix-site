02 · Capabilities

The whole production line, not one step of it

Making a comic drama is three jobs in a trench coat: writing one, drawing one, and running a channel. Most AI tools help with the middle job only. Runix Comic is designed to carry all three.

The story, before any pixels

Designed so a season starts as a plan, not a pile of prompts.

  • Script & episode planning — draft, restructure and break a story into episodes
  • Consistent characters — character sheets that hold across scenes and episodes
  • Storyboards — shot-by-shot boards generated from the script, editable before render

From board to finished episode

The middle mile: panels that hold a style, then motion and sound on top.

  • Styled panels — scene renders that keep one visual style across the episode
  • Motion & camera — pans, zooms and transitions that turn panels into video
  • Voice, music & subtitles — AI voice-over and multi-language subtitles

Shipped, then seen

An episode is not done until it is on a platform with a thumbnail worth tapping.

  • Export formats — vertical short-video cuts and full episodes from one project
  • Multi-platform publishing — package episodes for the platforms you post to
  • Promo assets — covers, trailers and titles/descriptions for each release
Runix Comic is in development. This page describes what is being built and the order we are building it in, not a shipped product. Waitlist cohorts get access as stages land, and what they say shapes what lands next.

03 · Fit

Who it is for

If you tell serialised stories in pictures, or want to start, this is being built for you.

Webtoon & comic artists

You already have characters and a style. Turn existing chapters into motion episodes without hiring an animation team, and keep the look that made the work yours.

Short-drama & content studios

Episode cadence is the business. Plan a season, keep characters consistent across dozens of episodes, and cut every release into the formats each platform wants.

Brand & marketing teams

Story-driven video that does not need a production house: a recurring cast, a consistent world, and promo assets generated alongside every episode.

07 · Questions

Common questions

What is Runix Comic?

An AI studio for comic dramas being built to cover the whole production line: pre-production, production, and publishing. Most tools stop at generating pictures; this one is designed around finishing and shipping episodes.

What does end-to-end cover?

Three stages: scripts, consistent characters and storyboards; then styled panels, motion, voice and subtitles; then export, multi-platform publishing and promo assets for each release.

When can I use it?

It is in development and not generally available yet. We onboard from the waitlist in small cohorts so early feedback actually shapes the product. Tell us what you make and we reply within one business day.

Who is it for?

Webtoon and comic artists moving into motion, short-drama and content studios producing serialised episodes, and brand teams that want story-driven video without a full animation pipeline.

Get in before the first cohort fills

Tell us what you make (a series, a channel, a back catalogue of chapters) and we will tell you honestly where the product is and when you would get access.
