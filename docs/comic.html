Comic: production workflow guide

On this page

  • 1. What you bring
  • 2. The three stages
  • 3. Working with the waitlist cohorts
  • 4. Joining

Runix Comic is an end-to-end AI studio for comic dramas, in development with a waitlist open. This guide explains how the production workflow is designed — what you bring, what the studio generates, and how early cohorts work with us while the product is being built.

1. What you bring

The studio amplifies material; it does not replace having a story. You bring a premise or existing chapters, a sense of your visual style, and — if you already publish — the platforms you ship to. Existing webtoon chapters are a strong starting point: adaptation is the shortest path to a motion episode, as covered in the format field guide.

2. The three stages

  • Pre-production — episode planning and script drafting, character sheets that hold across episodes, and storyboards generated from the script, editable before anything renders.
  • Production — styled panels consistent with your look, camera motion and transitions, AI voice-over and music, multi-language subtitles.
  • Publish & grow — vertical cuts and full episodes exported from one project, plus covers, trailers and per-platform metadata.

The full walkthrough of the craft — including where AI tools still fall short — is in How to make a comic drama with AI. We keep that honesty here too: character consistency and style drift still need human review, and the workflow is designed around checkpoints where you correct early instead of in the edit.

3. Working with the waitlist cohorts

Cohorts get access stage by stage as capabilities land, working directly with the engineers. What cohort creators say — where consistency breaks, which export formats matter, what the publishing step is missing — sets the build order.

4. Joining

Join the waitlist and tell us what you make: a series, a channel, or a back catalogue of chapters. We reply within one business day with where the product is and when you would get access.

Questions this page does not answer? Ask us directly — a real engineer replies within one business day.
