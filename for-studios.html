02 · Situation

The situation

Three things this team is usually dealing with when it calls.

Characters that drift between scenes

Image tools generate a panel at a time, and the same character comes back with a different face in the next one. Keeping one look across an episode becomes manual work that the tools were supposed to remove.

Pictures are not an episode

A comic drama needs a script, an episode plan, storyboards, motion, voice, music and subtitles before it is anything a platform will show. Stitching a chain of single-purpose tools together is a production job in itself, and each handoff loses something.

Publishing is where it breaks down

Each platform wants its own cut: vertical short videos for one, full episodes for another. Re-cutting by hand for every platform is the last step, and the step most often skipped.
