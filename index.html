02 · Quality

Official-grade quality, end to end

We connect only to official, first-party provider APIs — never resold or gray-market keys.

  • Official sources only — direct first-party endpoints, no reseller middlemen
  • Full model fidelity — official rate limits, no throttling or silent downgrades
  • Provider guarantees pass through — the upstream's data & compliance terms reach you intact
  • Low latency & 99.9% uptime with automatic failover across official endpoints
  • Consistent, auditable results you can trust in production

Why official-only matters: resold and gray-market API keys can be rate-limited, revoked, logged by third parties, or silently swapped to weaker models. Official sourcing is what makes both our compliance guarantees and our quality guarantees possible.
