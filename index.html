Live in minutes, not weeks

Runix speaks the OpenAI API. If your code already calls an LLM, switching is a base-URL change.

1

Point your client

Change the base URL to api.runixcloud.io and drop in your Runix key. The SDKs you already use keep working.

2

Choose a model

Call a model by id, or let routing pick based on availability and your configuration.

3

Ship with guardrails

Retries, failover, per-key limits, and usage tracking are part of the gateway from your first call.

How we handle your data

Straight answers, no marketing. For formal terms, see our Privacy Policy and Security page, or ask us for details.

You own your content

Prompts and outputs you send through the gateway are yours. We process them to serve your request — we do not use your content to train models, and we don't sell it.

Content vs. metadata

We keep operational metadata — usage counts, latency, and error codes — to run billing, reliability, and support. That's distinct from prompt/response content; see the Privacy Policy for what's kept and for how long.
