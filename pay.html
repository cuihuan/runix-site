05 · Before you

Before you pay

What happens between the payment and the plan or balance going live.

How am I billed?

Every plan is a monthly subscription in USD, charged by Stripe on the same date each month. Stripe emails a receipt for each charge. A top-up is a one-time payment with no renewal.

Which cards work?

Visa, Mastercard and China UnionPay. A card issued in mainland China needs international payments enabled on it first.

How do I cancel?

Email billing@runixcloud.io and we cancel it for you. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.

When does it start working?

We activate the plan or add the balance on your Runix account by hand and confirm by email within one business day of payment. Stripe's receipt arrives immediately; ours is the one that means it is live.

Can I move between plans?

Yes: write to billing and we switch you over, prorating what you have already paid for the month.

Do I get an invoice?

Stripe issues a receipt for every payment. Enter your business name and tax ID at checkout and they appear on it. For a formal invoice on your own billing cycle, talk to sales.

Not sure which plan you agreed?

Ask before you pay. A real person replies within one business day and tells you exactly what to pick here.
