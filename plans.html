Why there is no checkout

A checkout suits a product that works the moment it is paid for. Ours are configured for the team that uses them, so the conversation comes first and the wait disappears.

Set up by a person

Keys, limits, routing, deployments and contracts are configured by our engineers for the team that will use them, and confirmed to you in writing.

Priced to your workload

Quotes depend on the models, volume and deployment you actually need, so there is no rate card to pick from: the number comes in writing before you commit.

Paid the way your finance team pays

USD, prepaid balance or invoice, with itemised statements. Card payments go through Stripe or Airwallex, on a link we send once the plan is agreed.

Common questions

Is there a free evaluation?

For Runix Router, yes: evaluation credits are issued on request, with full router functionality and no card required.

Can we pay by card?

Yes, once the plan is agreed. We send a payment link: Stripe accepts UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard and Apple Pay, and Airwallex accepts cards, Google Pay and Afterpay.

Do you publish a rate card?

No. Quotes depend on the models, volume and deployment you actually need, and the rates are stated in writing before you commit.

Can we be invoiced?

Yes. Prepaid balance or invoice, in USD, with itemised statements; an MSA and DPA are available on request.

Tell us what you are building

The models, the data and the volume you expect. A real person replies within one business day with a plan and a price.
