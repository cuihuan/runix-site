After you pay

Your plan is activated on your Runix account by our team and confirmed by email within one business day. Stripe emails a receipt immediately — if you have not heard from us, or you need it sooner, reply to that receipt or write to billing@runixcloud.io.

Billing & receipts

Charged monthly to the card you enter at checkout. Every charge comes with an itemized receipt from Stripe. Enterprise customers can be invoiced on their own cycle, suitable for procurement.

Currency & tax

Prices are set in USD. Applicable sales tax or VAT is calculated at checkout or on your invoice where required. Nothing is charged beyond what your statement shows.

Cancel any time

Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. See the Cancellation Policy, and the Refund Policy for refunds.
