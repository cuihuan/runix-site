Prices are in USD and billed monthly by Runix AI Inc. Stripe may present the amount in your local currency at checkout — the amount shown on the Stripe page is the amount charged. Cards accepted include Visa, Mastercard, American Express and UnionPay. Subscribing means you accept the Terms of Service; cancellation is covered by the Cancellation Policy and refunds by the Refund Policy.

After you pay

Your plan is activated on your Runix account by our team, and we confirm by email within one business day of payment. If you have not heard from us, or you need it sooner, reply to the Stripe receipt or email billing@runixcloud.io and we will sort it out.

Billing

Charged monthly to the card you enter at checkout. Stripe emails a receipt for every charge. Manage or cancel the subscription from the link in that receipt, or ask us and we will do it.

Cancel any time

Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. See the Cancellation Policy.

Need something else?

Prepaid balance without a plan is on the pricing page. For volume, invoicing or a written agreement, talk to sales.
