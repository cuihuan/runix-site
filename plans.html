What happens when you press enter

01Plan

Runix maps the files in scope before it changes any of them, then writes the step list in plain language. You approve a plan you can read, not a black box.

02Edit

Multi-file edits with the diff in front of you. Every hunk is attributed to a step, so you can drop one change without unwinding the whole run.

03Verify

It runs your suite rather than summarising it. A failure feeds back into the plan and the agent tries again instead of handing you a broken branch.

04Ship

Branch name, commit message and PR body drafted from the plan and the diff, in the conventions the repository already uses.

Before you subscribe

How am I billed?

Every plan is a monthly subscription in USD, charged by Stripe on the same date each month. Stripe emails a receipt for each charge.

Which cards work?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and China UnionPay. A card issued in mainland China needs international payments enabled on it first.

How do I cancel?

Email billing@runixcloud.io and we cancel it for you. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.

When does my plan start working?

We activate it on your Runix account by hand and confirm by email within one business day of payment. Stripe's receipt arrives immediately; ours is the one that means the plan is live.

Can I move between plans?

Yes: write to billing and we switch you over, prorating what you have already paid for the month.

Do I get an invoice?

Stripe issues a receipt for every payment. Enter your business name and tax ID at checkout and they appear on it. For a formal invoice on your own billing cycle, talk to sales.
