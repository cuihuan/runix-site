How billing works

The details that matter — spelled out, not buried.

Billing models

Prepaid balance: you add credit up front and it is drawn down by measured usage (tokens/requests) on the models you call. That is the only self-serve model — there is no subscription and no monthly fee. Enterprise/custom: volume rates and invoicing by agreement.

Currency & tax

Billing is in USD. Applicable taxes (e.g. sales tax/VAT) are calculated at checkout or on your invoice where required. There are no hidden fees beyond what your statement shows.

Prepaid credits

Balance is drawn down as you use the Services and does not expire. Unused balance is refundable per the Refund Policy; usage already consumed is not.

Auto-recharge

Nothing renews on its own — there is no subscription to cancel. Optional auto-recharge (topping the balance back up when it runs low) is off unless you switch it on, and can be switched off at any time.

Overage

There is no plan limit to exceed. When the balance reaches zero, requests are refused rather than silently billed on. Per-key spending limits are yours to set, and we'll make the behaviour clear before you rely on it.

Cancel & refunds

Stop whenever you like — with no subscription there is nothing to cancel, and any unused balance is handled per the Refund Policy. The Cancellation Policy covers closing an account and what happens to the balance in it.

Invoices & receipts

Every payment comes with an itemized receipt. Enterprise customers can receive formal invoices on their billing cycle, suitable for procurement and accounting.

Payment options

Card and bank transfer are both available today through invoiced billing, set up with our team. Self-serve card checkout is being enabled now; it will run on a hosted, PCI-compliant page operated by a licensed payment processor, so Runix never sees or stores card details. Contact billing to set up an account.
