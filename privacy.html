Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information — and the rights you have over it.

Effective date: July 22, 2026  |  Last updated: July 22, 2026  |  Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Runix AI Inc ("Runix", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our websites, APIs, and services (the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — name or company name, email address, and similar contact details when you create an account or request services;
  • Business inquiries — contact details and the content of your message when you reach out to us;
  • Billing information — order references, transaction amounts, and invoicing details when you purchase paid services. Payments are processed by licensed third-party payment processors; we do not collect or store your full card number or payment credentials.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, access times, pages visited, and device information, used for security and service improvement;
  • Usage data — API call counts, token usage, latency, and error codes, used for billing, troubleshooting, and quality improvement;
  • Cookies — see Section 5.

1.3 Content you process through the Services

Data you submit to our APIs and pipelines ("Customer Content") is processed solely to provide the requested service. We do not use Customer Content to train models, and we do not sell it or disclose it to unrelated third parties. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights to the data you submit.

2. How we use information

  1. To provide, maintain, and improve the Services, including processing requests and handling billing;
  2. To verify identity, prevent fraud and abuse, and protect the security of the Services;
  3. To respond to inquiries and send service notices (such as invoices, incidents, or important changes);
  4. To perform internal analytics that help us improve reliability and product quality;
  5. To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We will ask for your consent before using your information for any purpose not covered by this Policy.

3. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers — such as cloud hosting providers and payment processors, strictly as needed to operate the Services, under contractual confidentiality obligations;
  • For legal reasons — when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Runix, our users, or the public;
  • In business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, in which case the receiving party remains bound by this Policy;
  • With your consent — in any other case, only after you explicitly agree.

4. Data retention and security

We distinguish between the different kinds of data we handle:

  • Prompt and response content (Customer Content) is processed to serve your request and is not used to train models. We minimize how long content is kept beyond serving the request; specific retention windows for a given service can be described in your agreement;
  • Operational metadata — such as request counts, token usage, latency, and error codes — is retained to run billing, reliability, and troubleshooting;
  • Security and audit logs — such as sign-in and access events — are retained to protect accounts and investigate abuse.

See our Security page for how we protect this data. In general:

  • We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it;
  • Data is encrypted in transit. Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis;
  • Sensitive fields are masked in analytics and support tooling;
  • If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the website functioning, remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand aggregate usage so we can improve the Services. We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate site traffic; the data we receive from it is not tied to a named account. You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work without them. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

7. International data transfers

We are based in the United States and may process information on servers located in the United States or other jurisdictions. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers of personal information.

8. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will provide prominent notice. Continued use of the Services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
