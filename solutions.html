02 · Teams

Six teams, one stack

Each page: the situation, the products and their roles, how it starts, what is in writing, reading, and questions.

For platform teams

One endpoint for every model, with the controls your team owns

For the team that owns the integration between the company's products and the model providers. Runix Router puts one OpenAI-compatible endpoint in front of every provider, with key custody, per-key limits and failover beneath it. Where training or inference data lives in object storage, Runix FS gives the GPUs a file system over the bucket.

For model teams

Data you can audit, a model you can measure

For teams that train or tune models and have to show where the data came from and whether the result beats what they run today. Runix Data builds training and evaluation sets with provenance per record. Runix Models fine-tunes open-weight models and serves them single-tenant. Runix FS reads it all from the bucket you already have.

For engineering organisations

AI coding rolled out, not smuggled in

For organisations adopting AI coding across many developers and many repositories, where a pile of personal accounts and no visibility is the current state. Runix Code, in development, is being built so every change lands as a reviewable diff, with seats, per-key limits and attribution by developer. Runix Router gives the model calls one policy and one bill.

For studios and creators

A studio for comic dramas, from script to episode

For studios and independent creators making serialised visual stories, comic dramas in particular, where the work is the whole production line and not a single picture. Runix Comic, in development, is being built around pre-production, production and publishing, as an independent agent with its own harness. Runix Router is optional in front of its model calls.

For regulated industries

Written answers for the review that never ends

For finance, legal and security teams that have to answer a reviewer before anything carries traffic. Runix Router puts the data terms, the contracting entity and the failover mechanism in writing. Runix Data applies domain rules for finance, legal and cybersecurity data. Runix Models serves a model single-tenant, in your cloud account, trained on your data and nothing else.

For research labs

Datasets in the bucket, GPUs that stop waiting

For AI for Science and embodied AI groups with datasets in object storage and records that need domain judgement before a model learns from them. Runix FS mounts the bucket as a file system, cached in front of the GPUs. Runix Data applies its rules for antibody, protein and embodied AI records. Runix Models tunes and serves open-weight models.

03 · How to read

How to read these pages

Statuses are literal

Router, FS, Models and Data are in early access; Pipeline, Code and Comic are in development. A solution page that names a product in development says so, and the page describes what is being built.

Nothing is invented

No customer names, no uptime numbers, no certifications. What is in writing is linked to the page that holds it: the terms, the privacy policy, the Security page and the pricing page.

One product is a fine start

The pages describe a path through the stack, not a bundle. Most teams begin with one layer and add the next when the first has earned it.

04 · Questions

Common questions

Do I have to buy the whole stack?

No. Every product works on its own and is bought on its own. A solution page names the products that usually fit one team; most teams start with one.

Which page should I read first?

The one closest to your job. Platform teams own the integration with model providers; model teams train or tune; engineering organisations are adopting AI coding; studios make serialised visual stories; regulated industries answer reviewers; research labs move datasets and models for science.

What if none of these is my team?

Write to us anyway. The pages are the six situations we see most, not a limit; a reply within one business day says which products apply and which do not.

Tell us which team you are

Your stack today, the thing you are trying to change, and the volume. A reply within one business day names the products that apply and the ones that do not.
