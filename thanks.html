What happens next

Two emails, then you are running

01

Stripe receipt

Already sent, to the address you entered at checkout. It carries the amount, the plan and a link you can use to manage the card on file. Keep it — replying to it reaches us.

02

Activation email from us

Within one business day. This is the one that means the plan is live on your account. If it has not arrived by then, write to billing@runixcloud.io and we will sort it out the same day.

03

Start running agents

The integration guide is in the docs. If you were already using Runix on a smaller plan, nothing changes except the capacity — the same keys keep working.

Paid the wrong tier?

Write to billing before the activation email lands and we will switch it without charging you twice.

Need an invoice?

Stripe's receipt covers most cases. For a formal invoice on your own billing cycle, tell sales what your finance team needs.

Changed your mind?
